🔑 Key takeaway from keyword analysis:
Why Freidin Brown wins the surgical-error cluster:
1. They named the query correctly. Title "Surgical Error Lawyer in Miami, FL", H1 "Surgical Error Attorney in Miami, FL" — singular, both variants, geo in each. Gallardo's plural "Surgical Errors Attorney" has 0 GSC impressions while the singular forms it omits carry 1,275
2. Twice the depth on a YMYL query: ~4,158 words vs ~2,014, and every H2 restates "Surgical Error" + "Miami". On medical-malpractice queries Google is unusually depth-sensitive
3. They shipped the FAQ schema: the only page of the four with FAQPage + Question + Answer. Gallardo wrote seven Q&As and marked up none of them
4. Quantified proof: $500M recovered, 300+ jury trials, since 1976, board-certified civil trial, named $38M/$32M verdicts. Gallardo's page contains no number a searcher can verify
5. The snippet does work: their meta description ends with a phone number; Quackenbush's opens with "Free Consultation - Call…". Gallardo's ends "protect your rights and get compensation" — no phone, no "free", no risk reversal, on a page converting at 0.03%
What Gallardo must add to win the cluster (pos 21.05, 3,021 impr, 1 click, 0.03% CTR):
1. Re-target the singular, and add "lawyer". This is the whole page in one fix. Rewrite title/H1/meta around "Miami Surgical Error Attorney" and work "surgical error lawyer" into H1-adjacent copy and an H2. Four queries at positions 9.24–21.51 and 1,275 impressions are waiting on a word the page never says
2. Mark up the FAQ that already exists. Seven Q&As are on the page in plain HTML. Wrapping them in FAQPage schema is a same-day change, and only one rival (Freidin Brown) would be competing for the rich result
3. Build the anesthesia-error section. 333 impressions across four anesthesia queries at positions 29–52 — the worst positions on the page — supported by a single bullet. That deserves an H2, 300–400 words, and its own FAQ entry
4. Put a number on the page. No verdict, no settlement, no case count, no review count as text, no attorney bio — against $500M (Freidin Brown) and $2.6B (FHVG). Also add a compensation/damages H2: all three rivals have one and Gallardo does not
5. Fix the two conversion leaks: delete priceRange: "$1500-$10000" (it contradicts the "No Win, No Fee" banner directly above it), and rewrite the meta description to lead with the risk reversal and carry (305) 261-7000 — the pattern every competitor on this SERP uses
