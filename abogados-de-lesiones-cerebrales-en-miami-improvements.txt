   -- CRITICAL: FALSE / INVENTED CONTENT --------------------------------------------------------

   A1. FAQ es2 "¿Cuántas personas tienen TBI?" - INVENTED MEDICAL TERMINOLOGY.
       Reads: "casi 1.7 millones ... cada año", "mas de 52000 mueren por una lesión cerebral TIPO 1",
       "aproximadamente 125000 quedan afectadas de por vida por una lesión cerebral TIPO 2".
       * "lesión cerebral tipo 1 / tipo 2" DOES NOT EXIST as a medical classification. It is not a
         translation error - there is no such scheme. TBI is classified leve / moderada / grave,
         assessed with the Glasgow Coma Scale.
       * The 1.7M / 52,000 / 125,000 figures are the CDC's 2002-2006 era estimates, ~20 years stale.
       Current CDC (cdc.gov/traumatic-brain-injury/data-research): ~214,110 TBI-related
       hospitalizations (2020) and ~68,663 TBI-related deaths (2023) - about 586 hospitalizations and
       190 deaths per day. NOTE: CDC has since moved the deaths figure to 2023; the English FAQ fixed
       on 2026-07-24 still says "2021", so the English entry now needs a small year update too.
       This is the exact twin of the English FAQ fixed on 2026-07-24 and left untouched in Spanish.
       File: App_Data/FaqEntities.xml (language="es", Service id="miami-brain-injury-lawyer").

   -- HIGH: LEGALLY WRONG OR OUT OF DATE SINCE THE 2023 TORT REFORM -----------------------------

   A2. COMPARATIVE NEGLIGENCE IS DESCRIBED UNDER THE PRE-2023 RULE. Article, "Causalidad" paragraph:
       "una persona o empresa puede ser acusada parcialmente culpable si tiene un porcentaje de
       responsabilidad según las leyes de negligencia comparativa de la Florida."
       That describes PURE comparative negligence. HB 837 (eff. 2023-03-24) added Fla. Stat.
       768.81(6): Florida is now MODIFIED comparative negligence with a 51% bar - a claimant found
       MORE THAN 50% at fault recovers NOTHING. The page never mentions the bar. A reader who is
       largely at fault is left with the opposite impression of the law.
       Exception worth stating on the page: 768.81(6) does NOT apply to medical-negligence claims.

   A3. STATUTE OF LIMITATIONS - RIGHT NUMBER, WRONG FRAMING, THREE DEFECTS. Article, "Límites de
       tiempo para reclamaciones de TBI en Miami":
       (i)  No accrual-date caveat. The 2-year period applies to causes of action accruing ON OR
            AFTER 2023-03-24. Claims that accrued BEFORE that date kept the old 4-year window. On a
            page live since 2018 whose own FAQ es7 invites people with years-old accidents, omitting
            this is materially misleading in both directions.
       (ii) "se cuenta desde la fecha en que el individuo sufrió su lesión" is wrong for medical
            malpractice, which the article itself lists as a claim type. Med-mal runs 2 years from
            the incident OR from discovery (Fla. Stat. 95.11(4)(b)), capped by a 4-year statute of
            repose, extended to 7 years for fraud/concealment, with a carve-out for minors up to the
            child's 8th birthday. The page gives one flat number for every claim type.
       (iii) "el tiempo límite ... es de APROXIMADAMENTE 2 anos". A limitations period is not
            approximate. Coming from a law firm this is the worst possible hedge.

   A4. FAQ es7 CONTRADICTS THE ARTICLE AND OMITS THE DEADLINE. "Tuve un accidente hace varios años.
       ¿Todavía puedo presentar mi caso?" is answered "eso no quiere decir que su caso se
       desestimará" with NO mention of any limitations period. It also contradicts A3(ii): the FAQ
       correctly notes people discover brain damage months later, while the article says the clock
       starts at the date of injury. One of the two has to change.

   A5. PREMISES LIABILITY OVERSTATED. Article, "Responsabilidad de las instalaciones": "si no es así
       el propietario de este lugar será juzgado como responsable por las lesiones del accidentado."
       Stated as a certainty. It also omits the actual controlling requirement - under Fla. Stat.
       768.0755 a claimant injured by a transitory foreign substance in a business establishment must
       prove the business had ACTUAL OR CONSTRUCTIVE KNOWLEDGE of the condition. The paragraph also
       addresses only commercial property; residential premises duties differ.

   -- MEDIUM: MEDICAL INACCURACY ----------------------------------------------------------------

   A6. FAQ es8 "¿Cómo diagnostican los médicos a alguien con TBI?" lists "rayos X" FIRST. Plain X-ray
       does not diagnose a brain injury - it images skull fracture. CT (tomografía) and MRI are the
       relevant imaging. The list also omits the Glasgow Coma Scale, the standard initial assessment.
       NOTE: the English FAQ en8 has the identical defect and was NOT caught in the 2026-07-24 pass.
       Fix both.

   A7. FAQ es1 "¿Qué es una lesión cerebral traumática?" - two problems.
       * "se atrofian las funciones normales de tu cerebro" - atrophy is a specific structural change,
         not what a TBI does acutely. It disrupts/impairs function.
       * "cuando recibes un golpe contundente en la cabeza" is too narrow: TBI also results from
         acceleration/deceleration without any direct blow, from penetrating injury, and from blast.
         The article itself contradicts this three sections later by describing a victim with no
         external bruising, and by listing "explosión de bomba" as a cause.
       This FAQ is also the answer that will be scraped for the "abogado de lesión cerebral
       traumática" AI Overview (720 vol / KD 1) - the highest-value keyword on the page. It needs to
       be right before it is promoted.

   A8. SYMPTOM LIST OMITS THE EMERGENCY DANGER SIGNS. The article's "Síntomas de una lesión cerebral"
       list is accurate as far as it goes, but leaves out the CDC "get medical help right away" red
       flags: one pupil larger than the other, repeated vomiting, a headache that keeps worsening,
       slurred speech, seizures, inability to wake up. For a page whose explicit advice is "vaya al
       médico", omitting the danger signs is a safety gap, not just an SEO one.
