🔑 Key takeaway from keyword analysis:
Why Dr. Afrooz outranks a longer, better-optimised Avana page:
1. 241 reviews at 4.9★ wired into AggregateRating + Physician schema — Avana ships neither, so no stars appear in the SERP
2. Ten FAQ answers promoted to real H2 headings (10 of his 24 H2s) — harvests People-Also-Ask and long-tail directly
3. Dual-geo title targets Miami and Coral Gables — a query Avana already surfaces for (7 impressions, pos 25.86) but never names
4. A $50,000 anchor matches what searchers expect a deep plane facelift to cost; Avana’s $5,800 reads as a different procedure
5. Seven embedded procedure videos plus named clinical staff (surgeon, APRN, aesthetician) — the E-E-A-T depth Google rewards on YMYL pages
What Avana must add to dominate "deep plane facelift miami":
1. Add AggregateRating + a Physician entity with the real Google review count — the single largest schema gap versus all three competitors
2. Promote the 14 existing FAQ answers from the accordion to real H2 headings — the content already exists, only the markup is wrong
3. Reconcile the $5,800 figure against the $25k–$60k market: show what it includes and what the $11,395 / $23,700 tiers add, or comparison shoppers bounce
4. Add geo sections for Coral Gables, Miami Beach and Fort Lauderdale — 28 impressions of proven 0-click demand already sit on those three variants
